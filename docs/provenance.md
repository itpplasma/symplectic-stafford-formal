# Symplectic Stafford research provenance

The private `itpplasma/symplectic-stafford` Markdown exposition, its working notes and proof graph, and this Lean repository are records of the same ongoing internal research development. The private exposition is not a separately published or presented prior source being formalized. Its dated chronology is retained for provenance, correspondence checking, and mathematical review.

For current release-preparation metadata, the project's own proof development is therefore recorded as `type: original-proof`, `relationship: other`, not as `original-proof/formalizes`. Genuine external sources such as Stafford's 1978 paper remain `background`. The Stafford38 Lean project is a related formalization/build dependency and is not duplicated as a mathematical source for the new symplectic proof development.

References to “paper”, “paper proof”, “paper source”, or “final paper” in older audit records, proof maps, module comments, and the TeX dossier refer to this internal exposition unless they explicitly cite an external publication. Historical audit files are retained rather than rewritten retroactively; this document and the current README/formalization metadata govern their provenance interpretation.

This clarification does not establish the universal higher-dimensional theorem, does not promote any M01–M11 gate, and makes no priority or publication claim. Phase I, Phase II, human review, release, and Palomar registration remain separate obligations.
